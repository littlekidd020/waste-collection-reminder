Keep your Auckland bin dates together. Confirm each bin’s schedule, see what’s next, and choose app reminders, widgets or calendar events.

## Description

Keep track of your Auckland household’s bin days with a schedule you can check and change.

Check your property’s dates on Auckland Council’s website, then set rubbish, recycling and food scraps collections separately. Choose a regular collection date and a weekly or fortnightly cycle for each bin. An address is optional; address search helps label your schedule and does not verify collection dates.

- See the next collection with all bins for that day together.
- Browse a month calendar or a list of upcoming collections.
- Move or skip an individual collection after checking a holiday or disruption notice.
- Choose evening-before and morning-of app reminders, with a visible reminder coverage date.
- Add a home screen or lock screen widget.
- Optionally add recurring collection events and reminder events to Calendar, or export a separate 90-day calendar file.
- Read Auckland sorting guidance with official source links. Videos load only when you choose them.

Saved schedules and written guides work offline. Source checks, Apple address search and optional videos need an internet connection. Collection dates and reminder times use New Zealand time, including when you travel.

App reminders cover a limited period. Open Bin Day NZ regularly to renew them and check the reminder status. Notification permission and your device settings affect delivery. Calendar events repeat from the schedule you saved; an exported file does not update automatically.

Designed for households using Auckland Council kerbside collections. Shared bins, private services and island collections may have different arrangements. The app does not automatically receive Council holiday or disruption updates, so check the official source and update your schedule when needed.

Your household profile is stored on your device. No Bin Day NZ account is required. Privacy and Help explains optional online services and how to report an app issue without posting your full home address.

## What’s New in 1.2

This release focuses on checking, editing and understanding your bin schedule:

- Set each bin’s regular dates separately, with individual moved or skipped collections.
- See grouped next collections, confirmation dates and reminder coverage.
- Use revised recurring Calendar integration and widgets that calculate from your saved schedule.
- Read corrected sorting guidance and access in-app Privacy and Help.

Please check your saved collection dates after updating. Address search does not set your collection schedule; confirm it against Auckland Council’s official information.
