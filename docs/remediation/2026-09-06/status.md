# Bin Day NZ — remediation status

**Audit began 6 September 2026 · verification updated 7 September 2026 · working-tree candidate 1.2 (3) · unpublished**

The [original audit](../../audits/2026-09-06/audit.md) is the pre-remediation baseline and remains unchanged. This record maps its findings to the current implementation. **Source repairs do not close the release gates: hold the next public release until the remaining checks are recorded.** No upload, submission, policy publication, recruitment or external outreach occurred as part of this work.

The public [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/bin-day-nz/id6761326182), checked during the audit, still records **1.1**. These changes describe the local **1.2 (3)** candidate, not the public binary. This is an uncommitted working-tree repair, recorded in the [source snapshot](evidence/source-snapshot.json). Existing user edits, historical screenshots and the older source ZIP were preserved; they are not release evidence for this candidate.

## Product and technical decision

Keep the native iPhone/iPad app: SwiftUI, SwiftData, MapKit/CoreLocation, UserNotifications, WidgetKit, EventKit, WebKit and String Catalogs. `project.yml` remains the build specification. No replacement framework, backend or new dependency was introduced.

The authoritative local input is now a **user-confirmed, per-bin schedule**. People check the official Auckland Council website, then enter regular collection dates and weekly/fortnightly frequencies. Address search is an optional Apple Maps label/location aid; it does not verify collection service or assign dates. Recurrence and reminder times use a Gregorian calendar in `Pacific/Auckland`. Explicit moved/skipped occurrences handle holidays and disruptions without changing the regular cycle. The app does not automatically discover later Council changes.

Removed the in-app Council scraper, inferred suburb/first-property assignment, bundled council schedule data, unsupported council picker and disconnected CloudKit update service. No application-level iCloud or cross-device account sync is claimed. The widget stores recurrence and exceptions rather than a finite list of future events. App notifications remain finite and expose a renewal horizon; optional recurring calendar events provide another reminder channel. Exported `.ics` files remain separate 90-day snapshots.

## Strategy and remaining release work

The [strategy audit](../../audits/2026-09-06/strategy.md) recommends a focused free Auckland utility while testing whether its setup, correction, reminders and accessibility offer enough value over Council tools and recurring calendars. This is a validation choice, not evidence that the free-community strategy is financially sustainable. Defer national expansion, paid tiers, accounts and a data platform until a specific need, maintained source access and an operating owner are established.

The [six-week pilot and maintenance record](../../pilot-and-maintenance.md) proposes 8–12 consenting households, a comparison task and explicit maintenance capacity. Record correct setup, app-induced errors, recovery, continued preference and actual support effort. It contains no recruited participants, measured outcomes, partners or funding commitments.

Before a release decision, complete the [release checklist](../../release-checklist.md): final build/tests and source snapshot; isolated calendar safety/permission/failure checks; cross-surface save/edit/relaunch/midnight behavior; real-device reminder and widget checks; current visual/accessibility and fluent-language review; signed-archive/privacy/metadata validation. Review the [unpublished store copy](store-copy.md) and [candidate privacy policy](../../privacy-policy.md) against those results. Keep unavailable evidence explicitly open.
