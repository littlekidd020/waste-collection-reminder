# Bin Day NZ privacy policy

Prepared 6 September 2026 and published 2 November 2026 as the App Store privacy policy link for Bin Day NZ 1.2. It describes the app as released; the address lookup uses Apple mapping services only and no Bin Day NZ server receives your data.

Bin Day NZ helps you keep a household bin schedule. You do not create a Bin Day NZ account. The app stores your chosen address, any saved coordinates, collection schedule and preferences on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this household profile, and we do not sell it.

## Address and location

When you search for an address, Apple mapping services process the search. Those requests leave your device. The app does not send your saved address to an in-app Council lookup API. Council source links open the official website externally; information you enter there is handled by Auckland Council. A network service can also receive technical information needed to handle a request, such as an IP address.

Using your device location is optional and requires iOS permission. You can enter an address without allowing device-location access. Typing three or more characters into the address field automatically requests suggestions from Apple's online address search. You enter and confirm the collection schedule yourself; it can be saved without a successful online address lookup.

## Saved schedules, widgets and reminders

Your schedule and preferences are kept locally. The app shares the schedule and selected language with its own widget through an Apple App Group. The widget does not need your full address to calculate the next collection. iOS handles scheduled local notifications and permission settings. The app does not send the household profile to a Bin Day NZ push server.

There is no application-level private iCloud sync or cross-device Bin Day NZ account. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings and are separate from app sync. Saved schedules can be read offline; new source checks and online content need an internet connection.

## Calendar access and exports

Calendar access is optional. If you enable it, the app requests permission to create and manage its collection events. It does not upload your personal calendar to the developer. Your calendar provider may sync events according to your calendar-account settings.

If you export a calendar file, you choose where to share or save it. The receiving app or service handles that copy under its own settings. An exported file is not a live subscription and is not changed when you later edit the schedule in Bin Day NZ.

## Optional videos and external links

Text guidance does not require loading a video. If you choose to load an embedded video, the app connects to YouTube's privacy-enhanced player using a web view without a persistent website data store. YouTube can receive playback requests and technical information. Privacy-enhanced mode does not mean no data is transmitted or processed.

Opening a Council, YouTube, GitHub or other external link connects you to that service. The service's own privacy practices apply, and any account already signed in there may affect how it handles your visit. See [Apple's privacy information](https://www.apple.com/privacy/), [Auckland Council's privacy policy](https://new.aucklandcouncil.govt.nz/en/privacy-policy.html), [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Control and deletion

You can change your address, schedule and reminder preferences in the app. iOS Settings controls location, notification and calendar permission. Removing a permission stops future access allowed by that permission; it does not delete files or events you already exported.

Choose **Remove saved data** in Settings to review deletion of the saved household profile, app reminders and widget schedule. The app first tries to remove events it can identify as its own. If calendar cleanup fails or permission has been revoked, **Remove local data only** lets you erase the household profile without granting calendar access and disconnects calendar sync. After local deletion succeeds, this choice also removes the saved calendar ownership identifiers. Existing calendar events may remain and continue alerting. Remove them in Calendar; Bin Day NZ will no longer track them.

Unmarked calendar events created by earlier app versions cannot be identified safely by this version. Review and remove old Bin Day events yourself in Calendar if they are no longer correct. Delete the app, rather than offloading it, if you also want to remove its remaining app preferences. Uninstalling does not remove calendar events. Delete exported files and shared copies in the apps where you saved them. Device backups and calendar-account copies are managed through your Apple or calendar provider settings.

## Support and policy changes

The developer is Kexin Li. Report app problems through [the project's GitHub issues](https://github.com/littlekidd020/waste-collection-reminder/issues). GitHub reports are public, and submitting one requires a GitHub account. Do not include a home address, exact device location, private calendar details or unredacted screenshots. Use a public sample or describe the problem without identifying your home.

If the app's data handling changes, update this policy and the App Store privacy information before releasing that behavior. The required-reason API manifests describe use of covered Apple APIs; they do not replace this policy or the App Store privacy answers.
