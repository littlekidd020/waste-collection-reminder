1. **2021.** Three cues drive that judgement: oversized, toy-like 3D illustrations; oatmeal surfaces with broad neumorphic shadows; and widely tracked uppercase captions paired with floating capsules. The translucent navigation looks newer, but the dominant aesthetic belongs to the early-2020s soft-interface period.

2. Five craft faults, ranked by harm:

**First, bottom-content legibility.** Schedule’s first “Next collections” entry and Guide’s lower lists become ghost text behind the tab bar. A top studio would adjust the resting composition and bar backing so text is either readable above navigation or cleanly concealed beneath it.

**Second, space allocation.** Today leaves a conspicuous gap between its explanation and the bins, then compresses the legend, next collection, permission button and navigation toward the bottom. Guide devotes an enormous area to one bin before reaching practical guidance. Reduce the illustration stages and establish a repeatable vertical rhythm.

**Third, dark-mode compositing.** Dark Today exposes pale wedges beneath the wheels and thin light contours around the bin bases; dark Guide shows the same fringe along the body and foot. Replace these cutouts with clean transparency and shadows rendered for the dark surface.

**Fourth, type hierarchy.** “Done for tonight” becomes dramatically larger than “Out tonight,” although the latter carries the urgent instruction. Guide introduces another headline treatment. Set consistent headline roles across screens and states; confirmation should not need substantially louder typography than the task.

**Fifth, material and colour.** Light-mode location and settings controls cast sprawling grey shadows, while their dark equivalents become faint outlines. The mint notification button also makes white lettering look washed out. Tighten the shadows, define a coherent surface hierarchy across appearances, and use dark lettering on mint.

3. **Yes, from the text.** “Out tonight” answers when, and the next sentence explicitly names recycling, rubbish and food scraps. Recognition slows because the instruction repeats itself, the illustration dominates the screen, and its legend is separated from the introductory copy. “Allow notifications” is the strongest actionable element, pulling attention away from the collection task. Nothing visible explains the swipe gesture.

4. In light mode, they mostly read as one physical set: overlapping bodies, compatible perspective and soft contact shadows provide a plausible shared floor. Their simplified surfaces look more like moulded miniatures than outdoor equipment, but that is stylistic. Dark mode does not hold up: the floor largely disappears while pale floor remnants remain, revealing the image boundaries. The bins become individually cut-out objects hovering over brown-black space.

5. The strongest moment is **completed Today**: “Done for tonight,” the explanation that reminders are off, and “Undo” establish the clearest relationship between state and action. The oversized heading still needs restraint.

The weakest is **dark Guide**: the solitary bin makes its pale cutout edge especially conspicuous, while useful guidance descends into the navigation’s muddy translucency.

6. Reduce Today and Guide’s illustration heights and rebalance their spacing so essential text sits comfortably above the tab bar.

Rebuild the dark bin imagery with clean edges, a consistent floor and believable contact shadows, removing every cream fringe and floor fragment.

Standardise headline sizes and control materials across screens and states, tighten light-mode shadows, and change mint-button lettering to deep green.
