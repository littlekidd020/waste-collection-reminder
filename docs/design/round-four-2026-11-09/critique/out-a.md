This looks designed in 2020: shaded spheres, diffuse shadows and cream pills recall peak neumorphism, with less disciplined hierarchy than the current [Tide Guide benchmark](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/).

First, the scale allocation is indulgent. Today’s enormous “10” dominates the actual bin instructions, while a conspicuous empty band separates the reminder button from the weekly summary. Guide repeats this imbalance: the giant red sphere gets more breathing room than the useful, tightly wrapped Yes/No lists.

Second, Schedule’s bottom chrome handles overlapping content poorly. The broad fade washes out the 24 November collection, while its coloured beads remain visible through the tab bar, competing with navigation. The heavy book silhouette also carries noticeably more visual weight than the outlined calendar.

Third, the reminder sheet lacks a convincing selection treatment. The green bead beside “7 pm tonight” repeats the visual language used for waste categories; meanwhile, grey text makes the other available choices resemble disabled options. A consistent trailing checkmark and stronger text contrast would clarify the hierarchy.

Keep the coloured bead vocabulary linking Today, Schedule and Guide. It gives an ordinary household task a tactile, recognisable identity; refine its lighting and scale while preserving that continuity.
