It looks designed in 2021: pastel neumorphism, shaded spheres and oversized serif numerals give it a concept-render aesthetic rather than the precise hierarchy of the [2026 reference apps](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/).

On Today, the hanging “glass” tag reads as opaque cream plastic. Its white outline, uniformly milky face and broad muddy shadow don’t establish convincing transparency or edge thickness; the coloured beads appear made from another material entirely. The mid-swing view makes that mismatch more conspicuous.

On Schedule, the large blank band between the hanging dates and “TUESDAY 10 NOVEMBER” disconnects the selected date from its collection details. The additional space before the first bin row compounds this: one selection feels like three separate compositions.

On Guide, the square tiles reserve most of their area for empty space between a small upper-left icon and a bottom-aligned label. Four simple categories consume almost the entire screen, pushing Recycling below the floating navigation. More compact proportions would improve scanning without losing the calm.

Keep the hanging-tag idea, especially its translation into the smaller Schedule tags. It gives an ordinary household chore a memorable physical metaphor and connects the screens through a recognisable object. That is the strongest foundation here; it deserves more disciplined surrounding craft.
